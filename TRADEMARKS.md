## You may, without asking

- Use "Conductor" to truthfully refer to this software and unmodified builds of it

- Distribute unmodified official releases, keeping the name.

- Use the name in article titles, tutorials, videos, package-manager formulae, and

## You may not, without written permission

- Distribute **modified** versions of Conductor under the names "Conductor",

- Use the marks in a way that implies your product, service, or fork is official,
  endorsed by, or affiliated with Monstrous Media.
- Use the marks (or confusingly similar names/domains) for commercial products or
  services, including commercial redistribution or hosted/repackaged offerings.
- Register domains, social-media handles, or package names that are confusingly similar
  to the marks.

## Logo use

The Conductor logo may be used unmodified to link to or refer to the official project.
Do not alter its colors or shape, use it as your own product's mark, or incorporate it
into your own logo.

Open a GitHub Discussion for borderline cases, or contact the maintainers privately for
commercial permission requests (see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) for channels). We aim to be
permissive with the community and strict only with confusion and misrepresentation.
